\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Does the structure of national mortgage markets shape how European
Central Bank (ECB) monetary policy moves house prices? I estimate panel
local projections for eleven euro area countries from 1999 to 2025, using
information cleaned high frequency policy surprises interacted with a
fixed versus variable rate classification of mortgage markets. House
prices respond negatively to tightening since 2009, and this result
requires the information correction. However, evidence that variable rate
countries respond more strongly is fragile. The differential is small,
changes sign across specifications, and is never robustly significant. A
cross country design alone cannot identify this channel.
\end{abstract}
