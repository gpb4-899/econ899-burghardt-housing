# econ899-housing-mp

ECON 899 MA research paper, Simon Fraser University.

Working question: do euro area housing markets react differently to the same
ECB monetary policy shock depending on whether a country's mortgages are
mostly fixed or mostly variable rate. The empirical design is a panel local
projection of real house prices on information cleaned ECB monetary policy
shocks, interacted with national mortgage market structure.

This file is the plain project description. The replication instructions are
in README.md.
